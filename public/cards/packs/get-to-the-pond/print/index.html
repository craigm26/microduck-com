Duck Cards › Get to the pond

Print the Get to the pond cards.

The five cards this pack plays with and no others, on one sheet. Untick any card you do not want, and it will not print.

  1. Print from your browser. Press Ctrl+P, or Command+P on a Mac. On an iPad or iPhone, use Share, then Print.
  2. Print at actual size, one side only. Choose Actual size or 100%, not Fit to page.
  3. Use card stock if your printer takes it. Plain paper works too. Sleeves made for ordinary playing cards fit, and make cards last a whole term.
  4. Cut on the grey lines. Each sheet holds up to three rows of three.
