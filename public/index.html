Get to know Studio ›

Room to experiment.

From the network that drives a duck to the motion you make your own. Here is what you can explore in Studio.

Policies

Load a trained .onnx, see what is inside it, and check whether it is one of Pollen's. Blend two into a third, or search one's weights on a bench, keep what comes out, and publish it to Hugging Face where every other phone's Community list can find it.

Motions

Write a motion keyframe by keyframe, or describe it in words and let a language model draft it. Every angle it writes is checked against the robot's own joint travel before you see it.

The bench

A phone has no physics engine. Point the app at a machine that does and it will run a policy or a motion properly, then tell you how often it worked rather than whether it looked good once. Put a network of your own on it under its own name and steer by it.

Challenges

Two published challenges, both open, both unmet. Score an entrant on the same grid the published numbers were measured on, edit it, and score it again.

The Lab

A life size duck on your floor in AR, synthesised duck calls, soccer, and a room capture that writes real scene geometry.

Evaluations

A run is an anecdote. Pick one of four presets, the thing being evaluated and a bench, and get back a log in inspect-robots' own format that somebody else can open and check.

Nobody has the hardware yet. Pollen Robotics' first Microduck deliveries are around Christmas 2026. Everything here runs against a trained policy on your phone, a physics bench on your own network, or a recorded motion. Nothing on this site is talking to a robot. The app can find a duck over Bluetooth and ask what it is, and with a small bridge running on the robot's own computer it can put a policy file on that computer's disk. It cannot drive one, and it says so on every screen where it matters.

Microduck Studio is an independent project. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Pollen Robotics. Microduck is their robot; this is an independent owner's app for it.

Policies released by Pollen are labelled as theirs throughout the app. Microduck Studio, duckkit and the bench are Apache-2.0. inspect-robots is MIT licensed, and this project adopts its log format and vocabulary without being part of it.
