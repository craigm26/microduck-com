Feedback

What worked, what did not, and what was unclear. Tell us; every message is read.

By email

Write to feedback@microduckstudio.com. Say which page, lesson, card or screen it is about, and add a screenshot if you can. Every lesson in the books has its own link that fills in which lesson it is.

Grown-ups: please write on behalf of children rather than asking them to email.

From the app

Testing Microduck Studio through TestFlight? Take a screenshot in the app and choose Share Beta Feedback, or send feedback from the TestFlight app. It arrives with the build and the device it came from.

In public

  • Something wrong on this site or in a book: an issue on microduck-com.
  • A bug in the app: an issue on duck-studio.
  • A published policy, a dataset or a challenge entry: the Community tab on its own page on Hugging Face.

Issues and discussions are public there, under your own account on that site.

What happens to it

Email to this address is forwarded to the developer's own inbox and kept there. Nothing you send is published without asking you first. This site still collects nothing: every link on this page opens your own mail app or another site.
