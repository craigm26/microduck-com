About this book

The Duck Who Stayed is a made-up story about Wren, her family's farm and Pip, a small walking robot. This is a first draft. Two chapters are here, they have not yet been tested with children, and the rest are still to come.

The story is invented. The Real robots panel at the end of each chapter is not: it describes how real machines work.

A note for parents

The book is meant to be read aloud. Pause before each Clonk and Bump, and children may start saying them with you.

Wren often says what Pip feels, and Aunt Mo answers, "We can't know that." A child who says Pip is glad has not got anything wrong. It is a good moment to ask what Pip actually did.

The Big Questions have no single right answer. They are there to be talked about.

If a child is near a real robot, a grown-up stays close by, and the child stays out of its path while it runs. Aunt Mo does the same in the story.
